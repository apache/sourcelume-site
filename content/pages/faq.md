## What is the Responsible AI initiative?

An Apache Software Foundation effort to strengthen the open source technologies that underpin
modern AI and to help ASF communities adopt AI responsibly. It provides access to AI models and
tooling, ecosystem support for AI-focused projects, and a place to share good practices. See
[Responsible AI](https://apache.org/foundation/initiatives/responsibleai).
